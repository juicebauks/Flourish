MK Studio


MK Studio’s metalwork brings a bold and distinctive voice to the world of botanical art. Known for blending industrial strength with organic inspiration, his pieces capture the textures and forms of nature in striking, enduring designs. The signature Tropical Shield series is a celebration of resilience and beauty, translating the sweeping curves and protective qualities of tropical foliage into sculptural works of art. Each piece is crafted with precision and creativity, offering not just decoration but a statement that connects natural wonder with contemporary design.
